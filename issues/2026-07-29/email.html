Real-time optical signal equalization with a silicon photonic spatially distributed reservoir computer

#04 · Tier 2 高质量正式论文 · Nature Communications · 2024-01-25
Toward grouped-reservoir computing: organic neuromorphic vertical transistor with distributed reservoir states for efficient recognition and prediction
Nat Commun：有机垂直晶体管产生 1152 个分布式 reservoir states，grouped-reservoir computing 实现 >94% 识别准确率和 >95% 预测相关。适合作为有机物理 reservoir 的器件参考。证据：可核验证据包括 ultra-short channel organic neuromorphic vertical transistor、distributed reservoir states、1152 states、temporal/spatial range ratio 2640/650、recognition accuracy >94%、prediction correlation >95%。
